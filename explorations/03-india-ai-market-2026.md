## Summary

India's AI/ML job market in 2026 is extremely active — driven by both global tech giants expanding their India R&D centers and a domestic product/startup ecosystem scaling fast. Salary data from Indeed India (May 2026), PayScale (May 2026), and LinkedIn live job listings reveals a wide compensation band from ₹3.6L (service co freshers) to ₹30L+ (senior at product/FAANG companies). GenAI/LLM skills are now the #1 differentiator in job postings; PyTorch is increasingly preferred over TensorFlow; and AWS leads cloud demand in India. The transition from services (TCS/Infosys/Wipro) to product companies (Flipkart, Swiggy, Razorpay, CRED) is the most impactful salary multiplier available to Indian ML engineers.

## 7. Key Trends & Strategic Observations (India AI Market, 2026)

1. **GenAI has replaced "classical ML" as the primary job title label** — "AI/ML Engineer" and "GenAI Engineer" now dominate listings; "Data Scientist" declining in relative prominence
2. **Agentic AI is the newest demand signal** — Coforge, EXL, and several startups explicitly hiring for "Agentic AI" frameworks (AutoGen, CrewAI, LangGraph)
3. **Bangalore remains the ML capital** but Hyderabad is catching up, especially for global tech R&D; Noida/Gurugram strong for finance-adjacent AI
4. **IT Services pivot to AI is creating volume but not depth** — Infosys, Wipro, TCS are mass-hiring for GenAI implementation roles, creating job quantity but skill ceiling concerns
5. **Kochi, Coimbatore, Jaipur, Ahmedabad** are genuine secondary AI hubs now, not just outsourcing cities
6. **Indian language AI (Indic NLP)** is government-backed and growing — highest growth potential for specialized NLP engineers
7. **Salary compression at bottom/top** — fresher salaries have risen (product cos pay ₹10–15L for top IIT/IISc grads) but mid-level inflation has slowed; the biggest gains are at senior+principal levels
8. **Remote-first culture retreating** — Most major companies returned to hybrid by 2025; pure remote is rare except for international clients
