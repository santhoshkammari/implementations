# DSA Requirements for AI/ML Engineering Interviews 2026

## Summary
ML Engineer interviews require DSA proficiency similar to SWE roles at FAANG (1-2 coding rounds), but total weight of DSA is lower since interview also includes ML coding, ML fundamentals, and ML system design. A top practitioner solved ~350 LeetCode problems at 55% Medium, 35% Easy, 15% Hard. Many companies do NOT ask LeetCode at all for MLE roles.

## DSA Weight: ~20-30% of total interview

## LeetCode Distribution
- 55% Medium
- 35% Easy  
- 15% Hard
- Total: ~350 problems recommended for FAANG MLE

## Time Allocation (3-month prep)
| Module | Time |
